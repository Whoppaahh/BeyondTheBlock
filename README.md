# 🧱 Beyond The Block

[![Minecraft Version](https://img.shields.io/badge/Minecraft-1.19.2-blue.svg)](https://www.minecraft.net/)
[![Loader](https://img.shields.io/badge/Loader-Fabric-important.svg)](https://fabricmc.net/)
[![License](https://img.shields.io/badge/License-MIT-yellow.svg)](LICENSE)

**Beyond The Block** is a comprehensive expansion for Minecraft 1.19.2, designed to breathe new life into your world with unique mechanics, powerful equipment, and beautiful new biomes. From advanced minecart systems to pet-focused enchantments, it pushes the boundaries of the vanilla experience.

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 🛠️ Advanced Mechanics
*   **Minecart Linking:** Create massive trains by linking minecarts together. Use **Shears** to unlink them.
*   **Theft Detection:** Villagers and guards are now more protective! Stealing from village containers might lead to consequences if you're witnessed.
*   **Optimized Performance:** Built-in enchantment caching and throttled world-tick processing ensure a smooth experience even with many active features.

### 🛡️ Equipment & Enchantments
*   **New Ores:** Discover **Miranite**, **Chromite**, **Nocturnite**, **Amberine**, and more!
*   **Custom Tools:** Use the **Xenolith Staff** to scan for ores or the **Trowel** for building.
*   **Unique Enchantments:**
    *   **Pets:** *Linked Inventory*, *Total Recall*, *Poison Resistance*, *Health Boost*.
    *   **Horses:** *Fleet Hooves*.
    *   **Combat:** *Phantom Slash* (teleport & bypass armor), *Life Steal*, *Temporal Slice*.
    *   **Utility:** *Timber Cut* (fells trees), *Smelting Strike*, *Shadow Mining*.

### 🌿 World & Blocks
*   **New Wood Types:** Explore **Cherry** and **Pale Oak** forests (backported features with custom twists).
*   **Functional Blocks:** 
    *   **Woodcutter:** For all your wooden needs.
    *   **Pet Bed:** A cozy home for your tamed friends.
    *   **Decrafter:** Reverse-engineer items back into their components.
    *   **Shrines & Pedestals:** Discover mysterious structures in the world.
*   **Cauldrons:** Enhanced cauldrons that can hold **Magma**, **Honey**, **Slime**, and **Ice**.

## ⚙️ Configuration

Beyond The Block is highly customizable. Using **ModMenu** and **Cloth Config**, you can fine-tune:
*   **Theft Ranges:** How far witnesses can see you.
*   **Performance Toggles:** Enable/disable enchantment caching and other optimizations.
*   **Gameplay Toggles:** Enable or disable specific mechanics to suit your playstyle.

## 📁 Development

If you wish to build the mod from source or contribute:

1.  Clone the repository: `git clone https://github.com/Whoppaahh/BeyondTheBlock.git`
2.  Setup workspace: `./gradlew genSources`
3.  Build JAR: `./gradlew build`
